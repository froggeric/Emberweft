# M6.7 — Vertical / Social Presets: Design

> **Status:** revised design, 2026-08-25 · **Slice S10.7** (standalone, pulled forward from
> roadmap M8's "Vertical/social media presets" deliverable) · Predecessor pattern:
> [M6.6 framing normalization](2026-08-17-m6.6-framing-normalization-design.md).
>
> **Owner scope decisions (2026-08-25):** formats = 720×1280, 1080×1920 (9:16),
> 1080×1350 (4:5), 1080×1080 (1:1) — no 21:9. Surfaces = one-shot export + flock
> archive; portrait previews and aspect-aware library thumbnails are out of scope
> (follow-ups). Approach = genome-level rotated framing (rotate the camera +90° and
> swap the normalization anchor). CLI tokens = descriptive names.
>
> **Post-review revision (2026-08-25):** a five-criterion adversarial review
> (clarity, architecture, resilience, acceptance, security) filed 33 findings;
> 32 survived refutation, 1 was refuted. The amendments are integrated inline and
> logged in §10. One approved decision changed substance — D3's rotation is now
> conditional on an authored-orientation mismatch (§3) — and awaits owner
> ratification with this revision.

## 1. Motivation

Social video is 9:16. TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts all want
1080×1920, and Instagram's feed portrait wants 4:5 (1080×1350). Today every
resolution surface in Emberweft is landscape: the named `Resolution` cases, the
GUI export sheet tiers, the CLI `--resolution` tokens (which silently maps any
unknown token to `.p1080`), and `ShardPresets.sensible`. A vertical export is
reachable only through `.custom(w, h)` — which the GUI sheet's steppers cannot
even express (step-16 arithmetic from seeds 1920/1080 can never land on
1080×1920), and which frames as an unrotated width-anchored canvas: the authored
composition in a horizontal band with extended — usually empty — field above and
below.

The owner's framing requirement: portrait output must carry **the same
normalization semantics** as M6.6 — the authored composition preserved exactly,
with the genome's authored aspect ratio becoming irrelevant to framing.

## 2. Background — why rotation is the right lever

**M6.6 recap.** A `.flam3` genome's `camera.scale` is absolute pixels-per-unit
authored for the genome's `size` canvas. `FlameKit.Framing.normalize` rescales
`scale × renderWidth / size.x` (width anchor, data-pinned: gen-248 `scale/width`
agrees to 2% across populations, `scale/height` differs 34%), applied at the
plan-construction choke points before interpolation. Landscape framing is a
solved problem; this design extends it to portrait.

**The pipeline is already orientation-blind.** One `pixelsPerUnit` serves both
axes (CPU `ChaosGame.swift:230-234`, Metal `Kernels.metal:2144-2148`); the grid,
spatial filter, density estimation, and display kernels are square and
aspect-independent; Metal dispatches are per-axis ceil-div. A 1080×1920 canvas
renders correctly today — only the preset tables are landscape.

**Camera rotation is exactly the right transform.** `Camera.rotation` (degrees)
is applied identically on both backends as a 2×2 rotation of the world point
after subtracting `camera.center`, before scale. Verified in code:

- `+90` rotates content clockwise (standard CCW matrix in a y-down grid).
- The pivot is always `camera.center`, which lands on the exact canvas midpoint
  at any aspect ratio. The model has no `rot_center` (the parser drops the
  attribute), so there is no pivot ambiguity to handle.
- Rotation interpolates **linearly** through transitions
  (`GenomeInterpolator.swift:74`) — baking +90 onto *both* endpoints of a pair is
  constant across the morph — and is untouched during loops (`Loop.swift` rotates
  only xform pre-affines).
- No modulo exists on the angle; +90 rides along through parse → render →
  serialize harmlessly.

**The anchor must swap.** On a rotated portrait canvas, plain width-anchor
normalization computes `scale × 1080/1920 = 0.5625×` — a 1.78× zoom-out. The
correct portrait rule anchors the **authored height** (the authored vertical
axis becomes the canvas horizontal axis after rotation):

```
portrait (landscape-authored genome):  rotation += 90°
                                       effectiveScale = scale × canvasW / authoredH
landscape (unchanged M6.6):            effectiveScale = scale × canvasW / authoredW
```

Exactness: a 1920×1080 genome on a 1080×1920 canvas gives factor 1080/1080 =
1.0 — pixel-for-pixel the authored composition, sideways, no resampling (the
chaos game projects directly onto the rotated canvas). The grid transposes
exactly, symmetric gutter included (`RenderTypes.swift:48-50`). For other
authored aspects the **anchored axis is still exact** and the mismatch lands on
the other axis, exactly as M6.6 already behaves in landscape:

| Authored | Portrait canvas | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 16:9 (1920×1080) | 9:16 (1080×1920) | exact fit, zero bands |
| 16:9 | 4:5 (1080×1350) | horizontal exact; 285px of the sideways composition cropped top+bottom |
| 4:3 (800×592) | 9:16 | horizontal exact; ~230px extended field top and bottom |

A square canvas never rotates: width anchor, exact horizontal, vertical
extension — today's rule.

**Encoder facts (probed on this machine, 2026-08-25, real `VideoEncoder`
configuration).** All four preset dimensions — including the even-not-mod-4
1080×1350 — encode cleanly on H.264 and HEVC. Odd dimensions are not rejected:
VideoToolbox **silently crops** them (1081×1921 encodes as a 1080×1920 track),
producing a frame/track mismatch with no error — the reason D9 fails fast at
parse time.

**Rejected alternatives.** Post-render pixel rotation of the RGBA buffer
produces identical pixels but must be inserted at every sink (one-shot encoder,
`--frame --png`, the archive's resolution-alignment choke point) and splits
render dims from canvas dims — two sources of truth for one fact.
Aspect-adaptive zoom-to-cover (the old `docs/playback/formats.md` sketch) crops
the authored composition and fights authorial intent — M6.6 §2.4 already
rejected that class.

## 3. The transform

Orientation is **derived from the final canvas dimensions, never stored**:
`height > width` → portrait, `width == height` → square, else landscape. Any
`.custom(w, h)` gets portrait semantics automatically.

**Rotation maps the genome's authored long axis onto the canvas long axis** — it
fires only on a mismatch. A portrait-authored genome (a user import; every ES
genome is landscape-authored) is already composed vertically and is never
rotated:

| Canvas | Authored shape (w vs h) | `faithful` | `normalized` |
|---|---|---|---|
| landscape or square | any | identity *(today's path)* | width anchor *(today's path)* |
| portrait | landscape (w > h) | `rotation += 90°` only | `rotation += 90°`; scale × canvasW / authoredH |
| portrait | non-landscape (h ≥ w) | identity | width anchor — already composed vertically |

- The conditional keeps `faithful` byte-identical to `animate` for
  portrait-authored genomes rendered at native dims (a portrait canvas), where
  an unconditional +90 would silently break the identity contract
  (`ExportCommand.swift:491-496` derives native portrait canvases; `animate`
  never rotates). It also keeps the normalized cell correct for them: an
  unconditional swap-anchor would apply a 0.5625× zoom-out **and** rotate an
  already-vertical composition sideways.
- A square-authored genome (h == w) takes the non-landscape row: no rotation —
  for it the two anchors are numerically identical, so rotation is the only
  difference and spinning a square composition is arbitrary.
- On a portrait canvas, rotation is the **preset's orientation semantic**, not
  part of the normalization choice: it applies in faithful mode too. "Authored"
  on a portrait canvas means *raw scale, sideways* — for the landscape-authored
  population, which is all of ES.
- `+90°` (clockwise) is a single named constant, arbitrary for abstract flames;
  flipping it is a one-line change if renders look better the other way.
- **One guard gates the whole transform, rotation included**: if `size.x ≤ 0`,
  `size.y ≤ 0`, or `scale` is not positive-finite, return the flame unchanged
  (the known gen-248 degenerate-header class; those renders are black on both
  backends regardless). `size` is `SIMD2<Int>` (`Genome.swift:179`), so the ≤ 0
  checks are total; the positive-finite phrasing covers the NaN-representable
  `scale`.
- The rule is relative to authored orientation and correct for any authored
  shape; it is not ES-specific.

**API shape (resolved).** A new pure sibling entry point —
`Framing.apply(flame:renderWidth:renderHeight:framing:)` — carries the whole
matrix. `Framing.normalize(flame:renderWidth:)` stays byte-unchanged for the
landscape-only surfaces. Six `Framing.normalize` call sites exist today; their
disposition:

| Site | Disposition |
|---|---|
| `ExportCoordinator.buildRenderContext` (`ExportCoordinator.swift:202`) | switches to `apply` (mode+orientation-aware; rotation reaches faithful portrait) |
| `ArchiveRenderer.unitFlames` (`ArchiveRenderer.swift:337-338`) | switches to `apply` — including faithful mode (today's guard returns `(A, B)` untouched at `:336`) |
| CLI `--frame --png` mastering path (`ExportCommand.swift:503-505`) | switches to `apply` |
| `PlaybackViewModel` (`:134`), `SequencePlaybackViewModel` (`:123`), `ThumbnailService` (`:91`) | stay on `normalize` — landscape-only preview tiers and fixed 1280×720 thumbnails (added by commit `2bed1ea83`, after the M6.6 spec) |

Applied before interpolation, so transitions blend rotated endpoints.

**Identity scoping.** The `faithful` ↔ `animate` byte identity holds on
landscape and square canvases and on portrait canvases whose genome is
portrait-authored (the matrix's identity cells). The pins use landscape
fixtures at genome-native dims (sierpinski 320×200) and are unaffected.

## 4. Scope

**In:**

- `ExportSettings.Resolution` named cases: 720×1280, 1080×1920, 1080×1350,
  1080×1080, added to the **existing** width/height accessors
  (`ExportSettings.swift:74-79` — switch exhaustiveness forces the arms).
- **Bitrates — one source.** A single pure pixel-band function in FlameExport
  (pixel count → tier Mbps per codec) that both `VideoEncoder.autoBitrate`
  (`VideoEncoder.swift:180-191`) and `ExportCoordinator.autoBitrateMbps`
  (`ExportCoordinator.swift:1411-1421`) delegate to, classifying named **and**
  `.custom` cases by pixel count so `vertical720` and `720x1280` agree. Landscape
  named values are unchanged (1280×720 → 25/40, 1920×1080 → 50/80, 2560×1440 →
  80/130, 3840×2160 → 150/240). **Documented side effect:** `.custom` dims —
  including the archive's aligned shard resolutions (`ArchiveRenderer.swift:260-261`)
  — move from the flat 50/80 fallback to their pixel-equivalent tier. Encode-only:
  frames, determinism, byte-identity pins, and catalog gates are untouched.
- GUI export sheet: four new tier entries — "Vertical 720p", "Vertical 1080p",
  "Portrait 4:5", "Square 1080". The framing picker is unchanged; the matrix
  defines what each choice means per orientation.
- CLI `--resolution`:
  - named tokens `vertical720 | vertical1080 | portrait4x5 | square1080`;
  - explicit `WxH` values → `.custom` with derived orientation;
  - **validation**: `WxH` must parse as two positive **even** decimal integers
    with `16 ≤ w ≤ 7680` and `16 ≤ h ≤ 4320` (the GUI steppers' bounds — the CLI
    is never more permissive than the GUI). Anything else — odd, zero, negative,
    non-numeric, out of range, malformed separator — exits 2 naming the violated
    constraint, alongside unknown tokens, in the `--fps`/`--framing` validated-flag
    style. Evenness is the encoder's 4:2:0 chroma requirement; odd dims are
    silently cropped by VideoToolbox (§2 probe), and `PixelBufferPool` traps on
    allocation failure (`PixelBufferPool.swift:43-44`) — parse time is the only
    clean guard. Do **not** require mod-16: the locked formats (1080, 1350) are
    not mod-16;
  - replaces the silent `.p1080` fallback (`ExportCommand.swift:701-707`).
- **Batch paths inherit everything** through the shared resolver
  (`ExportCommand.swift:805-815`, `ExportManager.swift:368`): a batch is
  single-resolution by construction (resolution is batch-wide), and a typo'd
  token now fails the whole batch at parse time instead of silently rendering it
  at 1080p.
- `ShardPresets`: the four portrait entries **join `sensible`** (8 members,
  ascending pixels with ascending-width tie-break for the equal-pixel
  720×1280/1280×720 pair). Membership matters: `preset(named:)`, the Settings
  default-shard picker, and the FlockView archive-dedup filter all read
  `sensible`; a sibling array would silently revert a user-selected portrait
  default shard to landscape 1920×1080 (`FlockView.swift:172-177`). The GUI's
  Standard/Vertical menu sections are derived by an orientation predicate
  (Vertical = `h > w`; Standard = landscape + square — square is unrotated per
  the matrix), not by array membership. `SettingsView.swift`'s default-shard
  picker gets the same grouping.
- **Catalog schema v4 — a version re-stamp, not an ALTER.** The framing gate
  values become `0` = legacy/faithful, `1` = normalized unrotated (landscape,
  square, or portrait-authored-on-portrait), `2` = normalized rotated. The
  `framing` INTEGER column has existed since v3 (base CREATE at
  `FlockCatalog.swift:84`; the v2→v3 ALTER at `:132`); 0/1/2 need no schema
  change. The migration is `UPDATE flock_meta SET value='4'` only; fresh
  catalogs seed at 4. The bump does two jobs: it refuses pre-M6.7 binaries the
  **whole catalog** (`schemaVersionUnsupported`, `FlockCatalog.swift:134-135` —
  a broader lockout than D8's checkpoint note, relevant for side-by-side dist
  builds), and it stops an old build from framing==1-MISS → re-render →
  upgrade-overwrite-clobbering gate-2 rows. While `migrate()` is touched, each
  multi-statement step runs inside `BEGIN IMMEDIATE … COMMIT` (the current
  non-transactional ALTER+version pair has a crash window that re-runs the
  ALTER and dies on a duplicate column).
- **Gate derivation — one shared source.** A single pure helper in FlameFlock
  (e.g. `FlockFramingGate.value(mode:canvasW:canvasH:authoredW:authoredH:)`)
  computes the gate per key: canvas dims come from the shard or row (never from
  `settings.resolution`, which is not guaranteed shard-aligned at the compare
  sites — the v0.6.0 default-resolution crash class), authored dims from the
  unit's A flame. All four consumers use it: `ArchiveRenderer` row write
  (`:307`) + mdta tag (`:407`), `StitchCoordinator` compare (`:220/:233/:262`),
  `GenerateCoordinator` compare (`:243/:246`).
- **Rebuild verifies file reality.** `FlockCatalog.rebuild` cross-checks the
  file's video-track `naturalSize` against the shard-parsed dims before
  upserting the row (the asset is already open in `readMdtaTags`,
  `FlockCatalog.swift:536-552` — one extra load). On mismatch: skip the row and
  log it, so the shard MISSes and re-renders at the correct path. This catches
  genuine portrait files misplaced across portrait shard dirs and
  backup-restored mixed trees — of any gate value, not just framing.
- mdta `emberweft.framing` carries the gate value (`"2"` for rotated portrait);
  rebuild parses it as today (`FlockCatalog.swift:495`).
- Flock browse-cell polish: `scaledToFit` so portrait thumbnails letterbox
  instead of cropping hard in the fixed-height cells (GUI-only, manual
  verification).
- Docs: roadmap M6.7 entry (M8 deliverable becomes a pointer),
  development-approach deferred-features note, README flag docs, CHANGELOG,
  `docs/playback/formats.md` (replace the adaptCamera sketch with a pointer to
  this spec), `docs/architecture.md` FlameKit Framing component (portrait
  branch + swapped anchor), CLAUDE.md framing paragraph (orientation-derived
  matrix).

**Out (follow-ups, not committed):**

- Portrait preview presets and aspect-aware library thumbnails/cards (16:9
  surfaces stay landscape; the preview popover offers only fixed landscape
  tiers — `PreviewPerfControls.swift:140-150`).
- 21:9 and other formats; HDR; duration/fps presets (defaults stay 30 fps,
  15 s loop / 12 s edge).
- GUI surface for faithful-portrait on the archive (CLI-only escape hatch).
- `rot_center` support (dropped by the parser today; irrelevant while the pivot
  is always `camera.center`).
- Export-sheet custom-dimension steppers reaching non-mod-16 dims (canonical
  presets cover the real targets; `.custom` + CLI `WxH` covers the rest).

## 5. Locked decisions

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | Genome-level rotated framing in pure `FlameKit.Framing` — not post-render pixel rotation, not zoom-to-cover (M6.6 §2.4). |
| D2 | Orientation is derived from final canvas dims (`h > w` portrait, `w == h` square), never a stored field; `.custom` inherits it. |
| D3 *(amended post-review)* | Rotation `+= 90°` (clockwise, one named constant) applies only when the canvas is portrait **and** the genome is authored landscape (`w > h`); it applies in faithful mode too. Portrait- and square-authored genomes are never rotated (identity / width anchor). |
| D4 *(amended post-review)* | Portrait normalized anchor = authored **height** (`canvasW / size.y`), only alongside rotation (D3). Square canvas and non-landscape-authored genomes: width anchor, no rotation. |
| D5 | Both landscape cells of the matrix are today's code paths, byte-identical — M6.6 pins hold by construction. |
| D6 | Defaults unchanged: `FramingMode` type-defaults `.faithful`; product entry points default `.normalized`; `animate` never normalizes or rotates (no new flags). |
| D7 *(amended post-review)* | Catalog framing gate: `0` legacy/faithful · `1` normalized unrotated (landscape, square, portrait-authored-on-portrait) · `2` normalized rotated; exact hit-gate; derived per key by ONE shared pure helper (canvas dims from shard/row, never `settings.resolution`). Schema v4 is a version re-stamp only — no ALTER (the framing column exists since v3); migration steps become transactional; rebuild cross-checks `naturalSize` before upserting. mdta `emberweft.framing` carries the value. A CLI faithful-portrait render records `0` — the owner-only mastering escape hatch, not orientation-distinguished (verified 2026-08-25: the live archive holds no portrait shards, only `1280x720_30fps` and `3840x2160_30fps`). |
| D8 *(amended post-review)* | New `Resolution` cases are one-way checkpoint-compatible; `.custom` round-trips as today. Failure modes stated: CLI `--resume` of an undecodable checkpoint fails clean at exit 2 with the `--discard` remedy (`ExportCommand.swift:247-253`); the GUI launch synth silently forgets it (remembered URL cleared, file left on disk — pre-existing D14 behavior, `ExportManager.swift:550-560`). |
| D9 *(amended post-review)* | CLI accepts `vertical720\|vertical1080\|portrait4x5\|square1080` + `WxH`; unknown tokens and invalid `WxH` (odd/zero/negative/non-numeric/out-of-bounds; positive even ints, 16…7680 × 16…4320) error with exit 2, no silent fallback. |
| D10 | The degenerate-header guard wraps rotation too — degenerate flames pass through entirely unchanged. |
| D11 | Portrait shards are new cache identities (shard name feeds `FlockSeed`): fresh seeds, no byte-reuse of landscape material — expected, not a bug. |
| D12 | Roadmap: standalone M6.7 / S10.7 pulled forward from M8; M8's vertical deliverable shrinks to a pointer. |
| D13 *(new)* | One pixel-band bitrate function in FlameExport; `VideoEncoder` and `ExportCoordinator` delegate to it; landscape named values unchanged; `.custom` reclassifies from the flat fallback to the pixel-equivalent tier (documented side effect). |
| D14 *(new)* | The four portrait presets join `ShardPresets.sensible` (ascending pixels, width tie-break); GUI grouping is derived by orientation predicate, never by array membership; `SettingsView`'s default-shard picker is updated. |
| D15 *(new)* | `rebuild` verifies each file's track `naturalSize` against its shard dims; mismatches are skipped and logged, never cataloged. |

## 6. Invariants preserved

1. **Determinism (rule #2):** the transform is pure; preset lists are fixed
   arrays; no hashed-collection iteration anywhere new.
2. **`animate` untouched:** no resolution/framing/orientation flags. The
   `animate` ↔ `export --framing faithful` byte identity holds on landscape and
   square canvases and on native-dims portrait canvases of portrait-authored
   genomes (the matrix's identity cells); the pins use landscape fixtures
   (sierpinski 320×200) and stay green.
3. **Engine parity untouched:** `git diff main -- Sources/FlameKit
   Sources/FlameReference Sources/FlameRenderer` shows `Framing.swift` only (the
   M6.6 invariant, extended to the portrait branch).
4. **Landscape framing unchanged:** all M6.6 framing tests pass unmodified.
5. **Stitch never mixes framing generations:** the gate is exact and
   single-sourced; a normalized portrait shard requires gate `2` per key, a
   normalized square shard gate `1`, and a legacy landscape row still HITs a
   normalized landscape request after the v4 bump.
6. **Resume stability:** settings ride `ExportCheckpoint` as today; orientation
   is re-derived deterministically from the resolution dims at re-normalization.

## 7. Architecture (file map)

- **FlameKit** — `Framing.swift`: the orientation-aware `apply` sibling
  (conditional rotation + swapped anchor) behind the degenerate guard;
  `normalize` untouched.
- **FlameExport** — `ExportSettings.swift`: four `Resolution` cases in the
  existing accessors + derived-orientation helper; new shared pixel-band
  bitrate function; `VideoEncoder.swift` and `ExportCoordinator.swift`:
  `autoBitrate`/`autoBitrateMbps` delegate to it;
  `ExportCoordinator.buildRenderContext` (`:201-203`): the framing call becomes
  mode+orientation-aware via `Framing.apply`.
- **EmberweftCLI** — `ExportCommand.swift`: token + `WxH` parsing with the D9
  validation; the `--frame --png` framing call (`:503-505`) switches to `apply`;
  `CLI.swift` help text.
- **FlameFlock** — `ShardPresets.swift`: 8-member `sensible` + orientation
  predicate; `FlockTypes.swift`: the shared gate helper; `FlockCatalog.swift`:
  v4 re-stamp migration (transactional steps), rebuild `naturalSize`
  cross-check; `ArchiveRenderer.swift`: `unitFlames` uses `Framing.apply`
  including faithful mode, gate value + mdta from the shared helper;
  `StitchCoordinator.swift` / `GenerateCoordinator.swift`: gate compares via the
  shared helper.
- **EmberweftUI** — `ExportManager.swift`: nothing functional (resolution
  already threads); test hooks as needed.
- **EmberweftGUI** (no test target, manual verification) — `ExportSheet.swift`:
  tier entries; `FlockView.swift`: shard-menu sections, browse-cell
  `scaledToFit`; `SettingsView.swift`: grouped default-shard picker.

## 8. Testing strategy

- **FlameKit framing pins** (`Tests/FlameKitTests/FramingTests.swift`): 16:9 →
  9:16 normalized = factor 1080/1080 = 1.0 + rotation 90; 4:3 → 9:16 anchor
  math; 16:9 → 4:5 = factor 1.0 + rotation 90 (horizontal exact; the top/bottom
  crop is a render-level consequence, not asserted here); faithful
  landscape-authored portrait = rotation only; portrait-authored on portrait =
  identity (faithful) and width anchor (normalized); square canvas = no
  rotation, width anchor; degenerate passthrough (NaN/negative scale,
  non-positive `size.y`) skips rotation too; landscape outputs identical to the
  M6.6 helper.
- **CLI parse tests** (beside
  `Tests/EmberweftCLITests/ExportFramingCLITests.swift`): each named token;
  `WxH`; unknown-token errors; malformed `WxH` cases (odd, zero, negative,
  non-numeric, out-of-bounds); batch resolution test mirroring
  `testBatchSettingsBuildHonorsFraming`.
- **CLI end-to-end mastering test** (mirrors the M6.6 precedent): run
  `export <sierpinski fixture> --segments 1 --frames 2 --frame 1 --png
  --resolution vertical1080` (and `--resolution 1080x1920`), assert exit 0 and
  PNG pixel dims 1080×1920.
- **FlameExportTests:** `Resolution` Codable encode→decode round-trip over the
  four new cases (the shape resume depends on); bitrate pin asserting encoder
  and precheck agree for every named case (including the four portrait ones)
  and the `.custom` twins (`720x1280` ≡ `vertical720`).
- **VideoEncoderTests:** one-frame round-trip sweep at 720×1280, 1080×1920,
  1080×1350, 1080×1080 × {h264, hevc}, asserting `.completed` and track
  `naturalSize` == requested dims (verified passing on this machine 2026-08-25;
  a cheap fleet-variance pin, `XCTSkipUnless(VideoEncoder.canEncode(...))`
  guarded).
- **FlameFlockTests:** v3→v4 migration asserts the version string with rows and
  framing values unchanged and **no ALTER fires**; cascading v1→v4; rewrite
  `testSchemaV4ThrowsUnsupported` (`FlockCatalogTests.swift:550-566`) to pin v5
  as unsupported; a legacy landscape row with framing == 1 still HITs a
  normalized landscape request after v4 (second-run hit-skip); a portrait row
  with framing < 2 MISSes a normalized portrait request; a normalized square
  shard HITs gate 1 and MISSes gate 2; the gate agrees across all four consumer
  sites (including a portrait-authored unit on a portrait shard → 1); mdta
  round-trip; a file whose `naturalSize` differs from its shard dims is not
  cataloged; `ShardPresets` pins updated (8-member `sensible`, ascending pixels
  + width tie-break, `canonicalDefault` by name not position,
  `preset(named: "1080x1920_30fps")` resolves with correct dims).
- **EmberweftUITests** (`ExportOrientationUITests`, mirroring
  `ExportFramingUITests`): `resolveSettings` threads a portrait-tier resolution
  (assert width/height) and the framing enum unchanged; the matrix transform
  semantics live in the FlameKit pins, not here.
- **Determinism + resume pins:** a portrait shard rendered twice yields
  identical frames; recommended: a 2-chunk portrait pause/resume byte-identity
  pin (also pins invariant 6's re-derivation).
- **Manual:** sheet tier entries, shard-menu sections + Settings picker, browse
  letterboxing, and a real A/B social render (rotation-direction taste check)
  before release.

## 9. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Sideways composition reads wrong on directional flames | A/B render real gen-248 sheep before release; direction and the rotation itself are one-constant flips; `suitable_for: vertical` seed metadata exists for future curation |
| Pre-M6.7 binary opens a v4 catalog: `schemaVersionUnsupported` refuses the **whole** archive (stronger than D8; side-by-side dist builds) | Accepted one-way break, same class as v1→v2→v3; the bump is what protects gate-2 rows from old-build clobber; upgrade the running build |
| A pre-M6.7 faithful-portrait row (gate 0, unrotated) HITs a faithful-portrait request whose fresh render is rotated | Gate-0 faithful portrait is orientation-undistinguished by design (owner-only escape hatch); accepted only because the live archive was verified portrait-free 2026-08-25. One-time remediation if one ever appears: delete the portrait shard dir (or `flock rebuild` + regenerate) before the first faithful-portrait run. Normalized (gate-2) requests are protected: 0 and 1 both MISS vs 2 |
| CLI faithful-portrait (gate 0) and GUI normalized-portrait (gate 2) alternated on one shard key overwrite each other per run | Pre-existing sanctioned in-place overwrite (`ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE`, `ArchiveRenderer.swift:331-333`); no wrong-HIT (0 ≠ 2 both ways), but each alternation re-renders every unit — do not alternate if the normalized cache matters |

## 10. Revision log (2026-08-25, post-review)

A five-criterion adversarial review (33 findings; 32 confirmed or partial, 1
refuted) produced these amendments:

1. **D3/D4 — rotation is conditional on the orientation mismatch.** The
   original design rotated every portrait canvas in faithful mode, which broke
   the `faithful` ↔ `animate` byte identity for portrait-authored user imports
   at native dims and double-distorted them under normalized. Portrait- and
   square-authored genomes now take identity/width-anchor cells. *This amends an
   owner-approved decision and awaits ratification with this revision.*
2. **D7 — schema v4 is a version re-stamp, not an ALTER.** The original
   "follows the v2→v3 ALTER-with-DEFAULT pattern" was unimplementable: the
   framing column has existed since v3. The bump's old-binary lockout
   (whole-catalog refusal) is now documented, migration steps become
   transactional, and `testSchemaV4ThrowsUnsupported` must be rewritten to v5.
3. **D7 — square's gate value defined: 1** (normalized unrotated). The original
   0/1/2 taxonomy had no slot for the square preset; write/compare divergence
   would have permanently re-rendered square shards.
4. **D7 — one shared gate derivation helper**, per key, canvas dims from
   shard/row (never `settings.resolution` — four call sites previously invited
   drift, and a misaligned request would silently re-render every run).
5. **D9 — `WxH` validation contract** (positive, even, bounded by the GUI
   steppers). Probe-verified motivation: VideoToolbox silently crops odd dims;
   `PixelBufferPool` traps on allocation failure.
6. **D13 — one pixel-band bitrate source**, replacing entries in two
   hand-mirrored dictionaries; `.custom` spellings now match their named twins
   (previously `720x1280` would encode at 2× `vertical720`'s bitrate).
7. **D14 — portrait presets join `ShardPresets.sensible`.** A sibling array
   would have silently reverted a user-selected portrait default shard to
   landscape 1080p; the exact-membership and positional-`canonicalDefault` test
   pins are updated accordingly.
8. **D15 — rebuild verifies `naturalSize`** against shard dims; misplaced files
   are skipped and logged rather than cataloged as silent HITs.
9. **§3 — all six `Framing.normalize` call sites enumerated with dispositions**
   (three preview/thumbnail sites were added after the M6.6 spec, by commit
   `2bed1ea83`); the open API-shape question is resolved as a sibling
   `Framing.apply` entry point, keeping `EmberweftUI` untouched.
10. **§3 — identity scoping stated**; §6 invariant 2 no longer asserts the
    faithful↔animate identity unqualified.
11. **§4 — batch behavior stated** (single-resolution by construction; parse
    errors now fail the batch instead of silently rendering at 1080p).
12. **§7 file map completed** (the framing call sites, the Settings picker, the
    shared helpers) and **§4 docs list extended** (architecture.md, CLAUDE.md,
    formats.md).
13. **§8 hardened:** malformed-`WxH` and batch parse tests; a CLI end-to-end
    portrait PNG mastering test; a `Resolution` Codable round-trip; an encoder
    round-trip sweep at the four dims × both codecs; a legacy-landscape-still-
    HITs post-v4 pin; square-gate HIT/MISS pins; a four-site gate-agreement
    pin; a `naturalSize`-mismatch catalog-skip pin; two §8 bullets reworded to
    assert only what their test layers can observe.
14. **§9 risks expanded** (old-binary catalog lockout, faithful-portrait stale
    HIT with remediation, 0↔2 cache thrash, `.custom` bitrate change,
    misplaced-file integrity).
15. **Refuted — no change:** the claimed NaN-`size` guard hole is impossible:
    `Flame.size` is `SIMD2<Int>` (NaN unrepresentable; the parser traps at
    `Int(Double.nan)`), and the spec's scale clause was already NaN-safe.

Two empirical probes (2026-08-25, this machine, real `VideoEncoder`
configuration) ground the design: all four preset dimensions encode cleanly on
H.264 and HEVC — including the even-not-mod-4 1080×1350 — and odd dimensions
are silently cropped, not rejected.
